Cancellation & Refund Policy

This Cancellation & Refund Policy applies to fees paid to SKYLabs India Private Limited, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 and operating the brand “Invictus IMC” (“Invictus”, “we”, “us”, “our”) through invictusimc.com. It should be read with our Terms & Conditions and Pricing page.

In short: a Bid Fee or Order Fee is non-refundable once your Bid or Order has been successfully recorded. If you were charged but nothing was recorded, or you were charged twice, we refund you in full.

1. What you pay for

Invictus charges a fee only when a User submits a new Bid (Manufacturers) or a new Order (Buyers), where a fee applies under our Pricing. The fee is for listing, processing and presenting your submission on the Platform. It is not a deposit or a payment for goods, and it does not guarantee that a Bid will be awarded or an Order will receive Bids. Editing a Bid or Order you have already submitted does not attract a further fee.

2. Cancellation

  • You can decide not to pay at the checkout step. If you close the payment window or the payment does not complete, your Bid or Order is not submitted and you are not charged.
  • Once a payment is successful and your Bid or Order is recorded, the service has been delivered and it cannot be cancelled for a refund. You may still edit the Bid or Order without charge, and Manufacturers may ask us to withdraw a Bid.

3. When you are entitled to a refund

4. When a refund is not available

  • Your Bid is not selected or awarded, or an Order closes with no award.
  • You change your mind, withdraw your Bid, or no longer wish to proceed after your submission is recorded.
  • Your Bid or Order is removed or your account is suspended because of a breach of the Terms.
  • The Order is closed or awarded to someone else after you submitted your Bid.

5. How to claim a refund

  1. Email connect@invictusimc.com within 7 days of the payment, from your registered email or mobile number.
  2. Include your company name, registered mobile number, the Razorpay payment ID (starts with pay_), the amount, the date, and a short description of the issue.
  3. We will confirm receipt within 2 business days and tell you the outcome within 5 business days.

6. How and when refunds are paid

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method (the same UPI ID, card or bank account used). Once we initiate a refund, it typically reaches your account within 5 to 7 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer. We cannot refund to a different account or method.

7. Failed payments

If money is debited from your account but the payment shows as failed or pending, it is normally reversed automatically by your bank or the payment provider within 5 to 7 business days. If it is not, contact us with the payment details and we will help trace it.
